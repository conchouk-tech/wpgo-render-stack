# WPGo Terms of Service — Draft for Legal Review

**Status:** internal draft only. This is not legal advice. It must be reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel before publication. Legal entity, governing law, consumer-law treatment, tax/VAT treatment, refund rules and liability language must be completed for the actual operating business and target markets.

## 1. Service

WPGo is software and hosted infrastructure that allows authorized AI/MCP clients to interact with a customer's WordPress site using defined WordPress abilities, approval controls and related account/licensing services.

The service may include:

- WordPress plugin functionality;
- remote MCP/OAuth connectivity;
- approval-gated actions;
- account/site management;
- commercial licences/usage quotas;
- billing integration;
- rendered-page/site-audit tools;
- signed software updates;
- support services.

## 2. Customer authority

The customer represents that they are authorized to administer every WordPress site they connect to WPGo and to instruct WPGo and connected AI clients to process or modify content on those sites.

Customers must not use WPGo to access or modify a site, account or data without permission.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- maintaining secure WordPress and AI-provider accounts;
- controlling who can approve WPGo connections and actions;
- reviewing requested write actions before approval;
- maintaining appropriate backups of their WordPress site;
- complying with applicable law and third-party rights;
- ensuring their use of connected AI providers complies with those providers' terms;
- protecting account credentials and promptly revoking access that is no longer required.

WPGo's approval system reduces risk but does not replace appropriate customer review, backups and access controls.

## 4. Acceptable use

Customers must not use WPGo to:

- compromise systems or accounts without authorization;
- distribute malware or malicious code;
- bypass access controls or security protections;
- unlawfully collect, expose or process personal data;
- infringe intellectual-property rights;
- conduct fraud, spam or abusive activity;
- intentionally overload or interfere with the service;
- resell or sublicense the service outside the permissions of their plan without written authorization.

WPGo may suspend access where reasonably necessary to protect customers, infrastructure, third parties or legal compliance.

## 5. Plans, quotas and licences

Features, connected-site limits and usage quotas may vary by plan. Current plan details should be stated on the pricing page or order form.

Commercial licences may be cryptographically signed and bound to authorized site hostnames. Circumventing licence/site restrictions is prohibited.

Founder/internal plans, promotional allowances and beta entitlements may have separate rules and should not be treated as generally available customer rights unless explicitly stated.

## 6. Billing — draft

Paid subscriptions may be billed monthly or annually through the designated payment provider.

Before publication, the final terms must define:

- prices and VAT/tax presentation;
- renewal rules;
- cancellation effective date;
- upgrade/downgrade proration;
- refund/cooling-off rights;
- failed-payment/grace-period behavior;
- consumer versus business-customer rights;
- currency and invoice process.

WPGo should not promise a refund policy until it has been legally reviewed for the markets in which the service is sold.

## 7. Beta / release-candidate services

Pre-release features may be incomplete, changed or withdrawn and may have lower availability than generally available features. Paying public customers should not be placed onto RC infrastructure unless the commercial beta terms expressly permit it.

## 8. AI-generated actions

WPGo does not control the reasoning or output of third-party AI providers selected by the customer. AI instructions may be inaccurate or inappropriate for a particular site.

Customers should review significant content, configuration, code and commerce changes before approval. WPGo may require explicit human approval for non-read-only operations but cannot guarantee that every customer instruction or AI recommendation is error-free.

## 9. Third-party services

WPGo relies on and/or integrates with third-party services such as WordPress hosting, AI providers, payment providers, cloud infrastructure and transactional email providers.

Third-party service outages, policy changes or account restrictions may affect WPGo. The final customer terms should clearly distinguish WPGo obligations from third-party obligations.

## 10. Updates

WPGo may provide signed software updates. Customers should install supported security/compatibility updates within a reasonable period.

WPGo may end support for obsolete WordPress/PHP versions or incompatible third-party configurations after appropriate notice where practical.

## 11. Availability and maintenance

Final public availability/SLA promises should not be made until production infrastructure and support coverage are established.

WPGo may perform maintenance and emergency security changes. Where reasonable, material planned maintenance affecting customers should be communicated in advance.

## 12. Security

WPGo uses technical and organizational measures described in its security documentation. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Customers must promptly report suspected account compromise or unauthorized WPGo activity and cooperate with reasonable containment steps.

## 13. Privacy and data processing

Processing of personal data is governed by the published Privacy Policy and, where WPGo acts as a processor for customer WordPress data, the applicable Data Processing Addendum.

## 14. Intellectual property

WPGo and its software/service materials remain the property of the operating entity/licensors except for rights expressly granted to customers.

Customers retain ownership/right to their WordPress content and other customer materials, subject to third-party rights.

A limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence should be granted to use WPGo for the subscription term and plan scope.

## 15. Feedback

Customers may provide feedback. Final terms may grant WPGo the right to use non-confidential feedback to improve the service without creating an obligation to implement it.

## 16. Suspension and termination

WPGo may suspend or terminate access for material breach, non-payment, security risk, unlawful use or abuse, subject to applicable law and any required notice/cure period.

Customers should be able to cancel subscriptions through the defined account/billing process.

On termination, access credentials/connections should be revocable and customer data should be handled according to the Privacy Policy/DPA/retention schedule.

## 17. Warranties and disclaimers — legal review required

The final terms should contain jurisdiction-appropriate warranty/disclaimer wording without attempting to exclude rights that cannot legally be excluded, especially for consumers.

Do not publish broad “as-is/no liability whatsoever” language without counsel review.

## 18. Liability — legal review required

Counsel must define:

- liability cap;
- excluded categories of loss where lawful;
- carve-outs for fraud, death/personal injury, confidentiality/data protection and other mandatory liabilities;
- consumer-law limitations;
- responsibility for customer-approved AI actions and backups.

## 19. Indemnity — legal review required

Any customer indemnity for unlawful content, unauthorized site access or third-party infringement must be proportionate and valid for the intended customer type/jurisdiction.

## 20. Governing law / dispute resolution

To be completed after confirming the operating legal entity, customer markets and whether the service is sold to consumers, businesses or both.

## 21. Changes

Material changes should be versioned and communicated with legally appropriate notice. Existing paid customers should not be silently moved onto materially worse contractual terms where law or contract requires notice/consent.
